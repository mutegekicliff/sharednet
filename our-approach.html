Our Process

From initial discussions to ongoing maintenance.

Step 01

Discuss the asset or connectivity requirement

We begin by understanding the specific needs—whether an operator requires capacity along a specific route, or an asset owner wishes to commercialise existing infrastructure.

Step 02

Survey infrastructure and establish condition

Our technical team conducts detailed surveys to map existing ducts, poles, and chambers, assessing their structural integrity and available capacity.

Step 03

Agree access arrangements and approvals

We define clear responsibilities, secure the necessary regulatory and municipal approvals, and establish transparent access agreements for all parties.

Step 04

Prepare shared capacity and onboard users

We undertake any necessary civil works—such as installing microducts or upgrading poles—to prepare the infrastructure, followed by the structured onboarding of service providers.

Step 05

Maintain records, manage access and coordinate maintenance

Ongoing management involves keeping accurate GIS records, processing access requests, handling billing, and coordinating proactive maintenance to ensure reliability.
