About Us

Developing a structured, wholesale-only approach to shared passive infrastructure in Uganda.

Our Focus

Sharednet Infrastructure Ltd is a subsidiary of Ron Outsourcing Ltd, established with a specific focus on shared passive infrastructure.

Our proposed operating model is wholesale-only and carrier-neutral. We aim to provide the physical foundation—such as poles and ducts—that different communications operators and utilities can share effectively.

"Carrier-neutral means infrastructure access is offered across operators, with clear terms and equal treatment."

By focusing exclusively on passive infrastructure, we do not compete with our users in the retail communications market. Instead, we provide a collaborative platform that reduces the need for duplicated construction, minimises civic disruption, and lowers the barriers to network expansion.

Our Proposed Role

Infrastructure Management

We intend to manage and commercialise existing service ducts and poles, ensuring they are utilised efficiently. This includes installing microducts to organise cables and create additional capacity within established routes.

Access Administration

We act as the administrative bridge between asset owners and operators. Our role involves managing infrastructure access agreements, transparent billing, collections, and detailed reporting to ensure accountability.

Maintenance & Records

Accurate data is critical. We propose maintaining detailed GIS records of ducts, chambers, poles, occupancy, and condition. We also coordinate the maintenance of shared infrastructure and the structured migration of cables from duplicated routes.

Partner with us

Whether you are an operator seeking network capacity or an asset owner looking to manage infrastructure access, we provide a structured approach.
