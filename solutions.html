Our Solutions

Structured approaches for developing, managing, and accessing shared communications infrastructure.

Shared Poles

We develop and manage poles designed to accommodate multiple operators. Rather than each provider erecting their own infrastructure, our model supports organised attachment positions and documented access arrangements.

  • • Clear structural guidelines for attachments
  • • Reduced visual clutter and street-level duplication
  • • Coordinated migration from existing duplicated poles

Service Ducts & Microducts

We manage and commercialise existing civil infrastructure. By installing sub-ducts and microducts, we organise cables and unlock additional capacity in existing underground routes without the need for fresh excavation.

  • • Comprehensive duct condition surveys
  • • Accurate capacity and occupancy records
  • • Professional microduct installation and infrastructure leasing

Fibre Installation & Leasing

Subject to necessary regulatory approvals, our intended fibre installation and leasing offering provides operators with immediate access to connectivity infrastructure, reducing their capital expenditure and deployment time.

We intend to roll out capacity in strategic corridors based on demand, offering dark fibre leasing to authorised operators on transparent, non-discriminatory terms.

Infrastructure Access Management

For asset owners, handling multiple access requests from different operators is complex and time-consuming. We provide comprehensive management of this process.

  • • Maintenance of strict access records

Asset Records & Maintenance

Reliable infrastructure requires meticulous record-keeping and proactive maintenance. We act as the custodian of infrastructure data and operational health.
