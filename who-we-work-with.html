Who We Work With

We provide a practical, carrier-neutral platform designed to serve the distinct needs of operators, asset owners, and utilities.

Communications Operators

Intended Audience: Mobile network operators, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), and wholesale carriers.

Their Requirement: Rapid, reliable, and cost-effective access to civil infrastructure to expand network coverage, increase capacity, and connect businesses or residential areas without the delays and capital expense of independent civil works.

Our Proposed Role: We provide structured access to shared poles and managed service ducts. By offering carrier-neutral capacity, we enable operators to deploy fibre quickly and maintain their networks efficiently, guided by transparent access agreements and clear maintenance protocols.

Public Asset Owners

Intended Audience: Government agencies, local authorities, and municipalities.

Their Requirement: Organising the deployment of communications infrastructure to reduce street clutter, prevent repeated road excavations, and effectively commercialise existing public ducts and civil assets.

Our Proposed Role: We act as the infrastructure manager, surveying existing assets, installing microducts to organise cables, and handling the administrative burden of multiple operator access requests. We ensure compliance with civic standards while generating structured reporting and revenue tracking for the asset owner.

Utilities

Intended Audience: Electricity distributors, water authorities, and organisations delivering connected public services.

Their Requirement: Safely accommodating communications cables on their utility poles or within their corridors without compromising the safety, maintenance, or reliability of their primary utility services.

Our Proposed Role: We propose managing the communications layer of utility infrastructure. This involves documenting attachment positions, ensuring structural compliance, coordinating maintenance windows, and acting as the single point of contact for operators seeking access to utility assets.

Infrastructure Owners & Technical Partners

Intended Audience: Private infrastructure developers, real estate managers, and technical service providers.

Their Requirement: Establishing carrier-neutral connectivity within private estates or business parks, or partnering to deliver specialised infrastructure solutions.

Our Proposed Role: We design and manage internal shared infrastructure for developments, ensuring multiple service providers can connect residents or businesses without duplicating internal wiring or civil works. We also engage with technical partners to deliver maintenance and installation services.
