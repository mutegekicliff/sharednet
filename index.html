The Carrier-Neutral Advantage

Carrier-neutral means infrastructure access is offered across operators, with clear terms and equal treatment.

By separating the ownership and management of passive infrastructure from the delivery of retail communications services, we ensure that every operator has fair access. This encourages market competition while reducing the environmental and civic impact of duplicated networks.
